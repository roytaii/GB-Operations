# Module Descriptions

## 1. Item Master

One page per product you sell.

- What it is, what brand, what category
- How it's packed. A case of shrimp is 20 bags of 2 lbs. 
- Size and weight of a case, and how many fit on a pallet
- Whether it goes in dry storage, the cooler, or the freezer
- How long it lasts
- What you pay for it, and whether it's sold by the case or by actual weight
- When to reorder — the point where you're getting low

Nuances: Different cases of shrimp from different suppliers will have separate pages/rows.

---

## 2. Customers & Vendors

One page per company you do business with.

### For a restaurant you sell to

- Who they are, and where to deliver — which is often not where to send the bill
- Who to call: the manager, and the person who handles payments
- How they pay you. 30 days, or cash at the door? 
- When you can deliver — some places won't take a delivery during lunch rush
- What the driver needs to know: use the back door, ring the buzzer, no big
  trucks on that street
- What prices they get, since not every customer pays the same

### For a supplier you buy from

- Who they are, where to send the payment
- Average duration it takes them to deliver after you order
- Their product numbers versus yours. They call it item 4471, you call it
  item B357A. 
- Copies of their food safety paperwork, and when it expires

---

## 3. Purchase Orders

A record of what you've ordered from suppliers but haven't
received yet.

- What you ordered, how much, at what price, from whom
- When it's supposed to arrive
- Whether it's drafted, sent, expected, received, matched, or paid

---

## 4. Receiving (incoming)

What actually showed up on the truck, and what shape it was in.

- Which purchase order the truck is against, and which vendor sent it
- The driver, the truck, and the seal number on the trailer
- What time it arrived, and which door it pulled into
- Who on GB crew checked it in
- How many cases arrived vs how many you ordered
- The actual weight
- Temperature when it landed: the trailer, and a probe stuck in the product itself
- Batch numbers, pack dates, and expiration dates off the cases
- Anything wrong (short, damaged, temperature, wrong item, expiring too soon) with a photo
- The barcode that goes on each case, and which spot in the warehouse it's headed to

Should match the PO

---

## 5. Inventory

What's in the building right now, and where.

- Every product, how many cases, in which spot
- Which batch it came from and when it expires
- Which zone it's in: dry, cooler, freezer
- Whether it's actually available, or damaged, or on hold, or already promised to someone
- A full history of every case: received, put away, picked, moved, thrown out — with who did it and when

---

## 6. Sales Orders

What customers have asked for but haven't received yet.

- Who ordered, what, how much, at what price
- When they want it
- Whether there's enough in stock to fill it
- Whether it's been picked yet, shipped, or is waiting on a backorder

---

## 7. Shipments (outgoing)

What physically went out the door.

- What was loaded on which truck, going to which stops, in what order
- Which driver
- What items were delivered
- Whether anything came back — refused, wrong item, damaged
- Proof of delivery: who signed, when

---

## 8. Parking / Dock Scheduling

Which truck is at which door, and what's coming, at what time.

- Suppliers' delivery appointments — who's arriving when
- Which dock door each truck is assigned to
- When your own trucks leave and come back
- Any trailers sitting in the yard

## 12. Reports

Pulled from everything above. No new information, nothing to type - read only.

- How old your stock is: 0–30 days, 31–60, 61–90, over 90
- How fast inventory turns over, against a target of about 30 days
- What's barely moving
- Gross margin: by product, by customer, by salesperson
- Who owes you money, and how late they are
- Which vendors are reliable: who ships short, who shows up late, who sends it warm
- How often an order can't be filled completely
- What's being thrown away, and what it cost
- Customers gained, customers still ordering, customers who quietly stopped
